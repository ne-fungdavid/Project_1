## Celebrity Selection and Identities

For the initial phase of the CelebA classification pipeline, I have selected a subset of five distinct identities. 
My selection process was governed by three primary criteria: visual diversity, demographic representation and 
dataset balance.

- ID: 7 - A. R. Rahman (24 images)
- ID: 797 - Aria Crescendo (25 images)
- ID: 2619 - Dmitry Medvedev (25 images)
- ID: 4428 - Jenna Fischer (23 images)
- ID: 7007 - Monika Brodka (24 images)

The chosen identities represent a wide spectrum of facial features to ensure the model learns robust, generalised
characteristics rather than specific demographic biases. By including individuals with varying hair colors (ranging
from blonde to black), different skin tones and diverse facial structures (encompassing a range of masculine and 
feminine features), we provide the network with a high-contrast training environment. This diversity can be crucial
for the model to learn "face-specific" features, such as eye shape and bone structure, rather than relying on 
easy-to-memorize features like hair color or skin tone.
